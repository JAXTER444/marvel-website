Engineering & manufacturing recruitment in South Africa

We provide engineering and manufacturing recruitment services for businesses seeking skilled professionals across technical, production, operational and management roles. From mechanical and electrical engineers to production managers and maintenance specialists, we connect employers with candidates whose technical expertise and experience align with their operational requirements.

    Roles we recruit in engineering & manufacturing

    Searches stay with the discipline and the environment. A mechanical engineer on a plant is a different brief from a production supervisor or a quality manager on a manufacturing line.

    • Mechanical engineers
    • Electrical engineers
    • Civil, structural, process, and industrial engineers
    • Instrumentation, automation, and control engineers
    • Production managers and supervisors
    • Plant and operations managers
    • Quality controllers and QA managers
    • Supply chain, warehouse, and logistics supervisors

    How we approach an engineering & manufacturing search

    We establish the technical discipline, project or plant requirements, and experience needed before beginning a targeted search for professionals whose skills and background align with your needs.
