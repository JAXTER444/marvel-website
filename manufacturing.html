Manufacturing recruitment in South Africa

We support manufacturing companies in finding skilled professionals across production, technical, operational and management roles. From production specialists and maintenance professionals to manufacturing managers, we help businesses recruit candidates with the practical experience and technical knowledge needed to keep operations running efficiently.

    • Production managers and supervisors
    • Plant and operations managers
    • Supply chain managers

    • Warehouse and logistics supervisors
    • Automation, robotics, and industrial control
