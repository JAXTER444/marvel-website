How our search procedure works

Thirty-two years of combined search and selection sit behind the work, across industries in South Africa. We build the relationship with both the candidate and the client, and we take the recruitment in hand so you can stay with the running of the business.

Brief

  1. Learn the culture

    We start with how the company works, then agree what the hire needs to achieve.

  2. Write the brief

    A detailed job description, so the search is aimed at the role you actually have.

  3. Agree the timeline

    We set when the person needs to be on board, before the search begins.

Search

  1. Search the market

    Advertise, search our network, and head-hunt where the role calls for it.

  2. Screen by phone

    A first conversation to test fit before anyone’s time is committed.

  3. Present the shortlist

    CVs come to you with a clear view of who is worth meeting.

  4. Arrange the interviews

    We set the meetings between you and the candidates.

Close

  1. Manage the process

    Both sides stay informed from the first interview through to a decision.

  2. Check on request

    Reference checks and psychometric tests, when you want them.

  3. Shape the offer

    We work with both parties so the offer is clear and agreed.

  4. See them through notice

    Guidance for the candidate from resignation through to the start date.

  5. Stay in contact

    We keep in touch with you and the new hire once they have begun.
