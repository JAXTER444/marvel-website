Mining roles we recruit

We support hiring across the mining sector, from technical specialists and skilled trades to operational and management positions.

  • Mining engineers and geologists
  • Metallurgists and surveyors
  • Plant, drill, and equipment operators
  • Diesel mechanics and electricians
  • Safety and training officers
  • Supervisors, superintendents, and mine managers

How we approach a mining search

A useful shortlist starts with the site. We ask how the team is structured, which shifts the role covers, and which qualifications are essential rather than preferred. Candidates hear the same detail before they commit time to an interview, and we stay involved after the placement so the start on site is clear.

From our Pretoria base we recruit for mining employers in Gauteng, the North West, and Mpumalanga, including site-based roles.

Mining recruitment questions

What do you ask before a mining search?

How the team is structured, which shifts the role covers, and which qualifications are essential. The roster and the tickets have to be clear before we introduce anyone.

Do you fill site-based roles?

Yes. From Pretoria we recruit for mining employers in Gauteng, the North West, and Mpumalanga, including site-based roles.

What do candidates know before they interview?

The same detail we took from the site: the roster, the tickets, and how the role actually runs.
