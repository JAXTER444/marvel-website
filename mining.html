Mining recruitment in South Africa

We specialise in mining recruitment, connecting mining companies with skilled professionals across operational, technical and management roles. From mining engineers and technical specialists to experienced mine managers, we help businesses find candidates with the qualifications, experience and industry knowledge needed to support their operations.

    We support hiring across the mining sector, from technical specialists and skilled trades to operational and management positions.

    • Mineral processing or metallurgy
    • Mining maintenance
    • Electrical, mechanical, or instrumentation

    How we approach a mining search

    Our mining recruitment process begins with understanding the operational environment, technical requirements and experience needed for the position. We then identify and assess candidates against the specific demands of the role, supporting the search through to appointment.
