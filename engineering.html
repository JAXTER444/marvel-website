Engineering recruitment in Gauteng

We provide engineering recruitment services across a range of technical disciplines, helping businesses find professionals with the skills and experience their projects and operations require. From mechanical and electrical engineers to specialised technical and engineering management roles, we connect employers with qualified candidates suited to their specific requirements.

    Engineering roles we recruit

    Searches stay with one discipline. A mechanical engineer on a plant is a different brief from a process engineer in a metallurgical circuit or an electrical engineer on a project.

    • Mechanical engineers
    • Electrical engineers
    • Civil or structural engineers
    • Mining engineers or metallurgical engineers
    • Instrumentation, automation, or control engineers
    • Engineering technicians, technologists, or draftspeople
    • Maintenance or reliability engineers
    • Engineering project managers
    • Design engineers and product development engineers
    • Plant, manufacturing, or industrial operations engineering

    Who hires engineers through us

    Mines, processing plants, EPCM firms, and civils contractors in Gauteng hire through this desk. The same employers also recruit for mining, construction, and SHEQ roles when the team around the engineer needs to be filled.

    How we approach an engineering search

    Engineering recruitment requires an understanding of the technical discipline, project requirements and experience needed for the role. We establish these requirements before beginning a targeted search for professionals whose skills and background align with your needs.
