Construction & mining recruitment in South Africa

We specialise in construction and mining recruitment, connecting businesses with professionals across project delivery, site operations, technical disciplines and management. From construction managers and quantity surveyors to mining engineers and operational specialists, we help companies find candidates with the qualifications, experience and industry knowledge needed to support their projects and operations across South Africa.

    Roles we recruit in construction & mining

    We support hiring across construction projects and mining operations, from site delivery and trades to technical, safety, and management positions.

    • Site managers, foremen, and project managers
    • Quantity surveyors, estimators, and commercial managers
    • Plant operators and skilled trades
    • Mining operations and mineral processing
    • Mining maintenance and instrumentation
    • Mine safety, occupational health, and environmental professionals

    How we approach a construction & mining search

    Every site and programme has its own demands—scope of work, shift pattern, and the experience needed on the ground. We take those into account when defining the brief, identifying suitable professionals, and managing the search through to placement.
