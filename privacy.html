Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Subject to legal review.

This policy explains how Marvel Placement Consultants (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you use marvelplacement.co.za or contact us. It is intended to align with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Please also read our Terms of Service.

Who is responsible

Marvel Placement Consultants is the responsible party for personal information processed through this website and related recruitment enquiries. We are a Pretoria recruitment consultancy and temporary employment service (TES) operating in South Africa.

  • Email: admin@marvelplacement.co.za

What we collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may process:

  • Contact details you submit on our contact form: name, email address, phone number (optional), organisation (optional), subject, and message.
  • Information you send us by email, phone, or in person about hiring needs or your career (for example role preferences, experience, and contact details).
  • Candidate application data when you apply for a role listed on our Jobs page. Applications are submitted through our third-party application platform (currently Cooptatio), not by uploading a CV to this website. That platform’s own privacy notice applies to data you enter there.
  • Limited technical information that your browser or hosting environment may generate when you visit the site (for example standard server or network logs). We do not run custom analytics or advertising cookies on this site today.

We do not ask for identity numbers, banking details, or CV uploads through the contact form on this site.

Why we process personal information

We process personal information to:

  • Respond to hiring or job-seeker enquiries.
  • Provide recruitment, search, selection, and related placement services to clients and candidates.
  • Share relevant candidate information with prospective employers where that is necessary for a placement.
  • Operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations that apply to our business, including labour and TES requirements where relevant.

Our lawful bases typically include your consent (for example when you submit the contact form), performance of a contract or steps you ask us to take before a contract, and our legitimate interests in running a recruitment consultancy—balanced against your rights.

How the contact form works

When you use the contact form, your browser opens a draft email (mailto) addressed to admin@marvelplacement.co.za with the details you entered. Those details are not uploaded or stored by a form backend on this website. Once you send the email, the information is received in our mailbox and processed as an enquiry.

Third parties

We may use or interact with third parties when providing our services, including:

  • Cooptatio — job applications and related candidate flows linked from our Jobs pages. Personal information you submit there is processed on that platform under its terms and privacy practices, and may be made available to us for recruitment.
  • Google Maps — the map embed on our Contact page is provided by Google. Google may process technical data according to its own policies when the map loads.
  • Clients — when you are a candidate we may introduce, we share information needed for the hiring process with the relevant employer or hiring manager.
  • Service providers — for example email, hosting, or IT providers that support our operations, under appropriate arrangements.

We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently set its own analytics or marketing cookies. Third-party content (such as the Google Maps embed) may set or read cookies or similar technologies according to that provider’s practices. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing, retention, and security

We share personal information only as described above, or where the law requires it. We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including recruitment cycles, client instructions, dispute handling, and legal retention requirements. Exact periods depend on the type of matter and applicable law; ask us if you need detail for your situation.

We take reasonable organisational and technical steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights under POPIA

Subject to the Act and its exceptions, you may request to:

  • Access personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request deletion or destruction of information where we are no longer entitled to keep it.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@marvelplacement.co.za or +27 12 030 0322. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Information Regulator contact details are published at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Our services are aimed at employers and adult job seekers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you should read the revised policy.
